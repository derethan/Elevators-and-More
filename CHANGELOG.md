## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Initial release of Elevators and More for Minecraft 26.3 (NeoForge).
- Industrial Guide Rails, Industrial Elevator Floors, Industrial Safety Railings.
- Elevator Control Boards for floor landings and Elevator Control Pedestals for carriage platforms.
